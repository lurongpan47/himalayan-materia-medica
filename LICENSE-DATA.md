## Image licenses (per-image)

Image licenses are **not** governed by this file. Each image carries its own `license` field in the plant entry YAML. Current accepted values:

- `PD` — Public Domain
- `CC-BY-4.0` — Attribution (any version 4.0 compatible)
- `CC-BY-SA-4.0` — Attribution-ShareAlike (same as data)
- `CC0` — No rights reserved
- `GFDL` — GNU Free Documentation License (Wikimedia legacy)

Images under any license that forbids commercial use, derivatives, or ShareAlike-incompatible terms are **rejected**.

## Why CC BY-SA

- Alignment with Sarasvatī (`~/clawd/Sarasvati/`), the sister canon-archive project.
- Prevents enclosure by downstream commercial databases.
- Allows academic use, teaching, remix for other open databases.
